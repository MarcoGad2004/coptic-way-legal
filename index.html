Coptic Way

Pray the hours, every day.

Coptic Way brings together the Agpeya, the Bible and the Coptic calendar, with its fasts and feasts. Set yourself a few habits of prayer and reading, and keep them alone or in a circle with family and friends. Private by design: we collect nothing, and there are no accounts. What you keep stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, and a circle sees only what you choose to share.

For iPhone and iPad. Free, with no purchases or advertisements.
